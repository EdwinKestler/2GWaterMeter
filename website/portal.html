Lab bring-up — one house, one meter

step 01
Start the stack

cd docker && docker compose up --build

step 02
Flash the Core+

Copy the IMEI from serial at 57600.

step 03
Register

Open http://localhost:3001 and register that IMEI plus your street address.

step 04
Expect “normal”

With a single meter the heatmap band reads normal — you are the median. That is expected.

Same database

Accounts are portal_users and portal_meters. Litres and fingerprints are SELECTs on the worker's readings and fingerprints. The reporting view is v_household_monthly.

Tables in play

portal_users
email, password hash, address
portal_meters
IMEI ↔ account, geofence point
readings
written by the worker, read by both UIs
fingerprints
one row per fit, per IMEI
v_household_monthly
reporting view
Deferred

Vercel is deferred. The portal cannot reach a private Compose Postgres from Vercel without exposing the database. Keep the portal container on the lab VM.
