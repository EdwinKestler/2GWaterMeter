Why the project exists

Autonomous urban water utilities

Empagua / Flatbox / MIT Solve: generate a household consumption signature from real flow, then detect leakage and overuse without waiting for a monthly bill.

The model

W(t) = w̄ + A₁ sin(2π t / T₁ + φ₁) + A₂ sin(2π t / T₂ + φ₂)

T₁ = 24 h, T₂ = 12 h, t = hour of day. Cloud fits the coefficients; the meter only evaluates W(t). That split keeps the ATmega644PA small and puts the “ML” (harmonic regression, not a neural net) in Python.

On the owner site (slide B)

If hour is 00:00–05:00 and live flow > W(t) + 0.15 L/min → lk = 1 (night leak).

Human-behaviour excess

Daytime flow > W(t) + 0.50 L/min → oc = 1 (overconsumption).

Pipe-network loss (slide C) needs more than one meter and stays in the cloud / Grafana.

What is in scope for the lab

  • One house, one IMEI, Docker Compose on a lab PC.
  • Same Postgres for MQTT ingest, fingerprints, and household login.
  • Household UI on the portal; Grafana for the operator.

2G end of life

The radio is a SIM800 on GSM/GPRS (original APN m2mgt.tigo.com). That was reasonable in 2016. A field product needs a carrier sunset letter and a path to LTE-M / NB-IoT or LoRaWAN. Hydraulics (RPE body + latching valve) can stay; sim800 and the MQTT client go together when the radio changes.
