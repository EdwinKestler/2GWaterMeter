Generate a household consumption signature from real flow, then detect leakage and overuse without waiting for a monthly bill.

The model

W(t) = w̄ + A₁ sin(2π t / T₁ + φ₁) + A₂ sin(2π t / T₂ + φ₂)

T₁ = 24 h · T₂ = 12 h · t = hour of day

The cloud fits the coefficients; the meter only evaluates W(t). That split keeps the ATmega644PA small and puts the “ML” — harmonic regression, not a neural net — in Python.

2G end of life

The radio is a SIM800 on GSM/GPRS (original APN m2mgt.tigo.com). That was reasonable in 2016. A field product needs a carrier sunset letter and a path to LTE-M / NB-IoT or LoRaWAN. Hydraulics — RPE body and latching valve — can stay; sim800 and the MQTT client go together when the radio changes.

Before you build

Do not buy SIMs or enclosures against this design without checking the 2G sunset date in your market first. Full 2G-EOL note on GitHub.
