Five numbers describe a household's day.

why two harmonics
Morning and evening, not one bell

A single 24 h sine cannot hold both peaks. The 12 h term costs two more floats and captures the second draw.

why on-device
The radio is off most of the time

Evaluating W(t) locally means a leak is flagged in the next 60 s publish instead of waiting for a round trip the modem may not get.

what it is not
Not a neural net

Least squares on seven coefficients. It fits in flash, it is inspectable, and a wrong flag can be traced to a number.

Overuse · oc = 1

Daytime and lm > W(t) + 0.50 L/min — human-behaviour excess against the signature.

EEPROM stores the last fingerprint, so validation continues after a reboot even with the radio down.
