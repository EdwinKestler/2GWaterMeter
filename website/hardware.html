Wiring (Core+)

External interrupts on this board are D2, D3, and D6. D2/D3 are SIM800 UART, so the Hall sensor is on D6.

water in --- RPE body + Hall --- water out
Hall out --> D6 (pull-up)   Hall V+ --> 5V   Hall V- --> GND
L9110 IA <-- D9 PWM         L9110 IB <-- D10 PWM
SIM800 UART <-- Serial1 D2/D3    PWRKEY D5    RESET D7
LED D13
