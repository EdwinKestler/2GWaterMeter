Household login, charts, and map — not Grafana

Grafana is excellent for operators. Households need IMEI registration, address geocoding, and a geofence. That lives in the Next.js portal on the same Compose Postgres.

Portal (resident)

Register with email + IMEI. Charts: hourly / daily / weekly / monthly. Overlay monthly W(t). Save address → OSM map, below/normal/above median.

Grafana (operator)

All SQL, heartbeats, every IMEI. Read-only. Do not send household users to :3000.

Lab (one house, one meter)

  1. cd docker && docker compose up --build
  2. Flash the Core+; copy IMEI from serial at 57600.
  3. Open http://localhost:3001 and register that IMEI plus your street address.
  4. With a single meter the heatmap band is “normal” (you are the median). That is expected.

Same database

Accounts are portal_users / portal_meters. Litres and fingerprints are SELECTs on the worker’s readings and fingerprints. Reporting view: v_household_monthly.

Vercel is deferred. The portal cannot reach a private Compose Postgres from Vercel without exposing the database. Keep the portal container on the lab VM.
